A separate complete fresh compiler replay, actual axiom-type audit, and six negative compile controls passed. The original R6 archive and construction data were unchanged. The general avoidance-profile calculus remains a written mathematical argument rather than a new general Lean theorem.

The author subsequently supplied an automated receiving-side review of the R6 package. It reports source, statement, evidence, and finite-replay checks, but did not rerun Lean. This is supporting automated review, not an independent human referee report. Its separate evidence archive is not included here.

The contribution depends substantially on the prior BPZ construction framework, base data, and Lean development. R3 combines existing BPZ rows at one node; R5 additionally changes recursive assembly and finite substitution/terminal tables while retaining the seeds and separation system. R6 verifies specific typed refinements within BPZ's arbitrary-alphabet framework. These releases do not claim the general recursive method as a new invention.

The model collaboration and these checks are not independent peer review or a substitute for independent statement and novelty assessment. Native evaluation and its compiler/runtime trust are disclosed in the build summary.
