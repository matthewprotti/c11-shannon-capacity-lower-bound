**Classification: AI co-developed.**

The contribution depends substantially on the prior BPZ construction framework, base data, and Lean development. R3 combines existing BPZ rows at one node; R5 additionally changes recursive assembly and finite substitution/terminal tables while retaining the seeds and separation system. Neither claims the general recursive method as a new invention.

The model collaboration and these checks are not independent peer review or a substitute for independent statement and novelty assessment. Native evaluation and its compiler/runtime trust are disclosed in the build summary.
